Backend developer jobs in Bangalore: FAQs

How many backend developer jobs are open in Bangalore right now?

There are 1,300 open backend developer roles in Bangalore on Weekday as of 3 October 2026, with 214 added in the last 7 days. The list updates daily.

What is the salary for a backend developer in Bangalore?

Most listings with a disclosed salary pay ₹18-45 LPA. Roles needing 2-4 years sit around ₹16-28 LPA, and staff or lead roles with 8+ years go to ₹55-95 LPA.

Which companies are hiring backend developers in Bangalore?

Grab, Coupang, Hiver, Endor Labs, SmartBear, HPE and Standard Chartered each have several open backend roles this month. Use the company filter to see only one employer.

Which skills do Bangalore backend jobs ask for most?

Java and Spring Boot appear in about 4 in 10 listings, followed by Go, Python, Node.js, AWS, Kafka and PostgreSQL.

Are there remote backend developer jobs for Bangalore candidates?

Yes. About 9% of these roles are fully remote within India. Pick Remote under work mode, or see all remote backend developer jobs in India.

Is Weekday free for job seekers?

Yes. Browsing, applying and AI apply are free for candidates. Companies pay Weekday to hire.
