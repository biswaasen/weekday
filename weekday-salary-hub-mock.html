Where these salaries come from

Every number on these pages comes from salaries shared by candidates on Weekday. We show the fixed salary (CTC minus bonus and stock), drop the top and bottom 5% to remove typos, and report the median and the middle 50%. Companies with fewer than 30 salaries are left out until we have enough data.

Want to know what a number means in your bank account? Use the in-hand salary calculator, or read CTC vs in-hand salary. Comparing roles rather than companies? See our 2026 guides for data scientists, full stack developers and product managers.

Salary FAQs

Which company pays the highest salary in India?

At 2–3 years of experience, Google (₹39.6 LPA median), Meesho and Apple (₹33 LPA) pay the most among companies with enough data on Weekday.

Which IT services company pays the most?

At 2–3 years, Accenture and Capgemini lead at ₹9.9 LPA median, followed by Infosys (₹9.7 LPA). TCS is ₹8.8 LPA.

Are these salaries CTC or in-hand?

They are fixed CTC per year, excluding bonus and stock. In-hand is usually 80–90% of CTC divided by 12.
