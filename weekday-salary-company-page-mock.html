TCS salary at a glance

TCS pays a median of ₹8.8 LPA fixed across 522 salaries reported on Weekday, with most people between ₹8.3 and ₹13.7 LPA. Freshers start around ₹8 LPA, and pay roughly doubles after 4 years, when people move into IT Analyst and lead roles. Data engineers and tech leads are the best-paid roles at TCS.

TCS salary FAQs

What is the average salary at TCS?

The median fixed salary at TCS is ₹8.8 LPA across 522 salaries on Weekday. The middle 50% earn ₹8.3–13.7 LPA.

What is the TCS salary for freshers?

Freshers (0–1 year) earn a median of ₹8.0 LPA, mostly as Systems Engineer or Software Developer. That is about ₹59,000 a month in hand.

Which role pays the most at TCS?

Data engineers (₹19.9 LPA median) and tech leads (₹18.3 LPA) are the best-paid roles with enough data.

How much does a TCS employee earn after 5 years?

With 4–6 years of experience the median is ₹17.1 LPA, about double the fresher median.

Does TCS pay more than Infosys?

At 2–3 years, Infosys pays a little more: ₹9.7 LPA median vs ₹8.8 LPA at TCS.
