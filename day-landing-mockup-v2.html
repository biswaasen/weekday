FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Day?

Day is Weekday's AI job agent on WhatsApp. You chat with it, it finds jobs that match you on Weekday's job board and applies to the ones you choose.

Is Day free?

Yes. Day is 100% free for candidates.

How does Day apply for jobs?

Day builds your profile from your chat and resume. When you say yes to a role, it submits your application on Weekday and keeps you posted on WhatsApp.

Will Day apply without asking me?

No. Day shows you matches first and only applies to the jobs you approve.

What jobs can Day find?

Roles on Weekday's job board across tech, product, design, data and more, in Indian cities and remote.

How do I start?

Tap "Say hi to Day", or save +91 90088 11889 and send "Hi" on WhatsApp.

Is my data safe?

Your details are used only to match and apply you to jobs. Ask Day to delete your data at any time.
