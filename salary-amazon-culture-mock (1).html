Amazon salary FAQs

What is the work culture like at Amazon?

In 3 anonymous reviews on Weekday, Amazon employees most often report 12–14 hrs workdays, weekend or after-hours work 'sometimes', and 5–10% hikes at appraisal time, with about 10 leaves a year in practice. 100% say a manager has never been rude to them.

What is the average salary at Amazon?

The median fixed salary at Amazon is ₹5.7 LPA, across 46,094 salaries shared on Weekday. The middle half of employees earn ₹4–11 LPA.

What is the Amazon salary for freshers?

Freshers (0–1 years) at Amazon earn a median of ₹4.2 LPA, and most get ₹3.5–6 LPA, most often as Associate, Software Development Engineer or Customer Service Associate. That is about ₹31,000 a month in hand.

Which role pays the most at Amazon?

Among titles with enough salaries, Software Development Engineer (₹20 LPA median) and Software Engineer (₹20 LPA) are the best paid at Amazon.

How much does a Amazon employee earn after 5 years?

With 4–6 years of experience the median is ₹5.8 LPA, and most earn ₹4.4–9 LPA. Freshers start at ₹4.2 LPA.

What does Amazon pay with 10+ years of experience?

People with more than 10 years at Amazon earn a median of ₹13 LPA, most between ₹7–21.4 LPA.

Does Amazon pay more than Oracle?

At 2–3 years of experience, Oracle pays 202% more: ₹13.6 LPA median against ₹4.5 LPA at Amazon. Comparing at the same experience keeps it like for like.

Are these Amazon salaries CTC or in-hand?

They are fixed CTC a year, before PF and tax, without bonus or stock. At the ₹5.7 LPA median, take-home is about ₹42,000 a month under the new tax regime.
